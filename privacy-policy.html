Privacy Policy — Net Finder

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Net Finder is a network diagnostic and traffic monitoring tool. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used. Net Finder is designed to work entirely on your device and collects no personal data. The only things it ever sends off-device are the network tests you start and the destination IP addresses it looks up to show country / ISP labels.

Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Captured app-traffic statistics stay on your device.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no user accounts.
  • Outgoing requests are limited to network tests you start (ping / speed test) and an automatic GeoIP + reverse-DNS lookup of the destination IP addresses the Traffic Monitor discovers.

Network data the app processes

Net Finder uses Android's VpnService API to create a local loopback VPN tunnel so it can observe the traffic produced by other apps on your own device. This is what allows the app to show which remote IP addresses and ports each app connects to.

  • Captured traffic is processed locally in memory to compute statistics.
  • Traffic is forwarded to its real destination so your internet keeps working.
  • Net Finder does not inspect, store, or transmit the content of your traffic (no messages, no credentials, no media).
  • Flow statistics and ping logs remain on your device and are cleared when you clear the app's data.

Outgoing network requests

Net Finder does not send your data in the background. The only requests it makes are the ones listed below, and none of them contain an account, contact, or personal information:

FeatureWhat leaves the device
Ping testICMP requests you run against a host you choose.
Speed testDownload/upload transfer to/from Cloudflare's public speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com) over HTTPS.
Traffic Monitor (automatic)For each newly seen destination IP, a DNS reverse lookup (PTR) and an HTTPS query to the ipwho.is service to show country / ISP / ASN. Only the destination IP being looked up is sent — never your traffic content or your identity.
Diagnostic report shareOnly when you tap Share — the report is sent through any app you pick.

None of these requests include your identity, contacts, or any app data.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number).
  • No precise location.
  • No contacts, files, photos, or messages.
  • No advertising ID.
  • No crash or usage analytics sent to a server.

Note: the GeoIP lookup described above sends only the destination IP address being looked up (a public server address) to ipwho.is. It does not send your device identity, your own IP as a profile, or the content of your traffic.

Sharing / export

The "Report" screen lets you export a diagnostic summary as text or PDF and share it using any app you choose. This action is always initiated by you, and the report contains only network diagnostics (device model, OS version, gateway/DNS, ping statistics, and remote IP addresses).

Children's Privacy

Net Finder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

All captured statistics are held only in memory / local app storage on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Since we collect nothing on our servers, there is no server-side data to delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
